\begin{center}
  {\LARGE\bfseries The Secretary Problem}\\[4pt]
  {\large The 37\% rule for picking the best}
\end{center}

You interview \(n\) candidates in random order and must accept
or reject each one on the spot. How do you pick the best?

\section*{The strategy}
Reject the first \(r\) candidates, then accept the first one
who beats all of them. The chance of getting the very best:
\[
  P(r) = \frac{r}{n} \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \frac{1}{i - 1}
  \approx -x \ln x, \qquad x = \frac{r}{n}
\]

\section*{The optimum}
\[
  \frac{d}{dx}(-x \ln x) = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x = \frac{1}{e} \approx 0.37
\]

\end{center}
